HOW TO READ THIS, AND HOW NOT TO.

The inflation factor is an UPPER BOUND. It is what a lidar-only detector's
recall must be multiplied by to express it over the complete annotated
in-range set, IF zero keyframe lidar returns implies non-detection. Multi-sweep
accumulation means some of those objects may still be detectable, so the true
correction lies between 1.0 and the figure shown.

What does NOT depend on that assumption: the removal criterion is defined on
range-sensor returns alone. It is correlated with lidar failure by construction
and uncorrelated with camera failure. Whatever the exact magnitude, the
direction of the bias is fixed, and it is largest at long range -- which is
where the camera-versus-lidar argument actually lives.

This is not a claim that any published number is wrong. It is a claim that
camera and lidar are not being scored on the same set of objects.
